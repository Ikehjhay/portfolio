AI Business Automation Platform

Agentix AI / Ilera  ·  co-founder, backend lead  ·  live
Platform

A multi-tenant SaaS platform that lets small businesses run customer support, catalog browsing, and order management straight out of WhatsApp. Built and live, co-built with a business partner from his product blueprint, and scaled to run multiple tenants in production.

  • Two FastAPI microservices on MongoDB, tenant isolated at the row level, talking directly to the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API with no third party BSP in between.
  • Per tenant RAG knowledge base, with a choice of LLM provider (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek) and vector store (ChromaDB or Pinecone).
  • React dashboard for business owners, with payment settings (Paystack, Flutterwave), PIN gated secret reveal, and self serve account claim.
  • Chased down production bugs most people would miss: a silent idempotency bug dropping WhatsApp messages, a MongoDB dot notation bug quietly wiping payment secrets, a timezone bug crashing message handling.
